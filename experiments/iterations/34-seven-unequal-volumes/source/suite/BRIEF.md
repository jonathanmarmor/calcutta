# Calcutta: Seven Unequal Volumes — public generation specification

User request: “Let's make another but make it much more varied in terms of number of movements and durations, and durations of the larger volumes. Let's do 7 volumes this time. You can make individual mp3s for each movement, in addition to a full length mp3 and ones for each volume.”

Plan recorded during generation:
- Seven volumes remain inside the earlier 20–80 minute range while varying much more: 20:00 / 1 movement; 27:30 / 7; 41:00 / 3; 23:00 / 11; 72:00 / 2; 34:00 / 5; 58:00 / 4. Thirty-three movements range from 15 seconds to 46:48. Volume durations total 4:35:30. A full master adds six four-second inter-volume gaps: 4:35:54. Inside each volume, movements are separated by two seconds; there are 26 such gaps, totaling 52 seconds.
- Deliver all 33 movement MP3s, seven continuous volume MP3s, and the full master. Volume and master MP3s have movement chapter metadata. Add a complete listening index, individual links, source bundle, public instructions, scores, MIDI and audits.
- Use fresh deterministic seeds and an eight-event theme per work, with larger-act density/dynamic shaping in long movements and short scene/ensemble changes (under 30 seconds). Different movement profiles include arch forms, intimate chamber counterpoint, chaconne, consonance graphs, phrase clocks, relay cues, chorale, concertino, trills, sustained fields, inversions of density/register, singing, glissandi and effects. Long works receive five larger acts above the scene layer; actual per-scene acts are recorded in the scores.
- Continue the earlier fixed-frequency acoustic/electric/analog/FM palette, prompt dry attacks and releases, quiet dissonance, fifth/triad/seventh subsets, independent phrase clocks and recognizable sequences. No click, detuned/chorused voices, default loud surprise stabs or global ending fade.
- Preserve exact original Calcutta pitches for all pitched notes except movement 11 (An Ordinary Amen: a deliberate tonal 12-ET piece, ending F-to-C) and movement 26 (The Slope Is Continuous: continuous smooth slides between exact field endpoints, ending by separating from unison).
- Movements under 90 seconds are genuine field postcards: they select fields 1,3,7,12,21,38,39 rather than rushing through all forty. Other Calcutta works traverse all forty fields via advancing contiguous neighborhoods. Every Calcutta movement starts beyond 1:2 and spends its final 40% in field 39. The early 1:2 visit is deliberately shortened even in long works. The ET movement uses inherited scheduling labels without claiming those labels are its harmony.
- Original wordless formant singing occurs only in movements 7 and 30. The final breath of movement 30 is explicitly unpitched breath synthesis. No copied singer or human-voice service. Effects occur only in movements 15 (reversed grains) and 28 (finite multi-tap echoes). All other works are dry. Event retrograde/mirror techniques reverse the note order, not the audio. Trill foregrounding is concentrated in movement 10; discrete note alternation is not vibrato.
- Thirty-three ending specifications in plan.py/endings.py: different answers, omissions, reciprocal gestures, unrollings, interrupted thoughts, releases, reprise transformations, temporal expansions, isolated tones and missing responses, plus special vocal, trill, gliss and effect exits. Each is an explicit event score rather than a shared appended fragment.
- Save original pitch input, inherited compact models/license/provenance, new code, all generated note/voice/cue/phrase decisions, act/section ensembles, endings, vowel/glide/effect settings, actual tempo grids, MIDI, validation and regeneration checks. These are public generation instructions, not hidden reasoning.

Implementation details and limits:
- The source instruments are the inherited sample-derived fixed-frequency resynthesized models. They are not untouched samples or newly installed native synths. Four original formant voice identities supplement the 97 inherited source colors.
- Audio is rendered at 24 kHz stereo. Individual and volume listening files are 48 kHz / 256 kbps MP3. The complete 4:35:54 master is encoded at 48 kHz / 192 kbps to keep it a manageable single file; it is encoded directly from PCM, not from the MP3s.
- MIDI reuses nonoverlapping lanes of the same instrument to stay within the MIDI port count on the longest works; original phrase/part identity remains in each score. Continuous glides have 20 ms bend samples in MIDI and continuous integration in audio. Custom instrument/voice synthesis and post effects are represented by source code and settings, not standard MIDI messages.

Repository transport adjustment: GitHub create_blob returned HTTP 422 for the longest original MP3 payloads (input too large). `archive_listening.py` encodes repository-only copies over 40 MB directly from PCM at 128 kbps, or 96 kbps for the longest movement to stay below the API payload ceiling. Delivered individual/volume files stay at 256 kbps, and the full master at 192 kbps. `suite/archive_audio.json` records both delivered and archive hashes/bitrates.
