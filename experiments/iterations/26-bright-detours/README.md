# Calcutta: Bright Detours

8:14. Eight exposed analog/FM synth leads; 46 episodes changing every 7–14 seconds; slow songs, rapid hockets, unequal phrase cycles and mixed-tempo inventions.

[Listen](calcutta_detours.mp3) · [MIDI](calcutta_detours.mid) · [Listening guide](source/calcutta_detours/calcutta_detours_guide.md) · [Original brief](BRIEF.md) · [Frozen source](source/) · [Source ZIP](source.zip) · [Manifest](manifest.json)

The new lead patches put open ladder-filter saw, resonant sequencer, Oberheim pulse/brass, TAL hollow/reed and Dexed glass/organ sounds in the foreground. Twelve individual sampled acoustic/electric instruments and two occasional composite instruments provide contrast. Twenty texture types contrast long sustained melodies, slow canons, fast hockets and bursts, and independent unequal phrase cycles. Foreground attacks remain prompt and dry. No click, added ambience, or master fade.

All forty original fields occur as selected subsets; every note remains exact. This piece prioritizes distinct inventions rather than restating all 32 construction pitches at every change.
