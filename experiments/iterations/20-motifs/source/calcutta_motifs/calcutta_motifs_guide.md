## Texture ideas

- **Song:** A returning turning melody above a soft triad; its answer begins before the accompaniment finishes.
- **Interwoven:** Two root families alternate and overlap, with contrary melodic replies and common tones carried through.
- **Seventh Garden:** A slow seventh rests over changing triads; an upper line explores the available nearby pitches.
- **Two Roots:** Two internal chords ask and answer, then their notes interleave in a brief conversation.
- **Canon:** A short motif reappears a quarter note later in another register over a nearly motionless accompaniment.
- **Chord Melody:** A foreground melodic route connects changing internal triads, with soft broken-chord replies.
- **Suspension:** One existing pitch is held while the other chord members change underneath, then the melodic thread answers.
- **Quiet Trios:** Two softly blended trios take turns, with an exposed line emerging between their entries.
- **Invention:** A small ascending subject and a descending answer share unequal rhythmic shapes; the subject returns with a changed ending.
- **Farewell:** Earlier motifs and their two root families return as quiet, widely spaced fragments.

## Reproduce

Run from the archive root:

```sh
python calcutta/motif_choirs.py
python calcutta_motifs/render.py
```

Dependencies: Python, numpy, mido, pedalboard 0.9.26, FluidSynth, ffmpeg, Surge XT supporting patch format 31, Dexed, and OB-Xf 1.0.3. Plugin paths are in the renderer and `calcutta/mixed_synths.py`. SoundFonts: TimGM6mb and GeneralUser GS (https://schristiancollins.com/generaluser.php). Third-party binaries/sample banks are not bundled. Configure `FLUIDSYNTH_LIBRARY`/`LD_LIBRARY_PATH` as needed. The score, fixed calibration, source field pitches, source palette, and plugin states are included.

MIDI has 51 tracks on four ports with independent ±2-semitone bends. It does not embed plugin states or the audio master dynamic envelope; the JSON and renderer describe the supplied recording.
